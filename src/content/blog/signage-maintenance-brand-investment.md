Your corporate signage represents a significant investment in your brand. Without proper maintenance, even the highest quality signage deteriorates, diminishing its impact and potentially damaging your brand's reputation.

## The Cost of Neglect

Faded colours, flickering lights, damaged panels, and dirty surfaces signal neglect to customers. They subconsciously associate the condition of your signage with the quality of your business. A well-maintained sign, on the other hand, reinforces professionalism and attention to detail.

## Essential Maintenance Practices

### Regular Cleaning
Dust, pollution, and bird droppings accumulate on signage surfaces. Regular cleaning — monthly for illuminated signs, quarterly for non-illuminated — keeps your signage looking its best.

### Electrical Inspections
LED signage requires periodic electrical checks to ensure consistent illumination, identify failing power supplies, and prevent fire hazards.

### Structural Integrity Checks
Monsoon winds and weather exposure can loosen fixings and damage structural components. Regular inspections catch issues before they become safety hazards.

### Illumination Testing
For illuminated signs, test all lighting elements regularly. A single burnt-out LED in a channel letter can significantly impact the sign's appearance and brand perception.
