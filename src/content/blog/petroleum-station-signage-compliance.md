Petroleum station signage in India is subject to stringent regulatory standards set by the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas, the Bureau of Indian Standards, and individual oil marketing companies (IOCL, HPCL, BPCL). Compliance is not optional — it is a legal and safety requirement.

ㅤ

## **Regulatory Framework**

ㅤ

Petroleum retail outlets must adhere to several key standards:

ㅤ

- **OISD Standards** — Oil Industry Safety Directorate guidelines covering all aspects of station design and safety signage.

ㅤ

- **BIS Standards** — Material and fabrication specifications for signage components.

ㅤ

- **OMC Brand Guidelines** — Each oil marketing company has detailed brand manuals specifying colors, logo placement, typography, and illumination levels.

### **Key Signage Requirements**

### **Canopy Signage**

ㅤ

Must display the OMC brand identity with precise color specification (Pantone-matched), installed at minimum height clearance, with uniform illumination across the entire canopy fascia.

ㅤ

### **Price Boards**

ㅤ

Digital or manual price boards must display current fuel prices legibly, with regular updates. Board dimensions and placement follow OMC-prescribed specifications.

ㅤ

### **Safety and Warning Signs**

ㅤ

Mandatory signs include:

ㅤ

- "No Smoking" warnings
- "Switch Off Engine" instructions
- Fire extinguisher location markers
- Emergency exit signage
- Maximum fill level indicators

ㅤ

### **Pole and Pylon Signs**

ㅤ

These must comply with structural safety standards, wind load calculations, and electrical safety for illuminated variants.

ㅤ

### **Why Professional Installation Matters**

ㅤ

Non-compliant signage can result in:

ㅤ

- Regulatory penalties and fines
- Delay in station commissioning
- Brand inconsistency across the network
- Safety hazards leading to accidents
